How to use this checklist

Work through each section in order and tick items as you go. Take your time, since an inspection is not a race. Tap ⚑ Flag on anything that worries you and + Note to jot details. Your progress saves automatically on this device.

Ask the seller to have the unit on shore power, with water, propane and fuel available, so every system can be tested live. If you look at more than one unit, use Reset (or the "Copy Report" button first) so details do not blur together.
