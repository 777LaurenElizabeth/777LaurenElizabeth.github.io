The walkthrough

  1. Pack the bag. Use the Items to bring list below so you have everything in hand when you arrive.
  2. Confirm what the seller will have ready. Check the Items to have list below, ideally before you drive out.
  3. Open the checklist and fill in the top card. Add the unit, seller, date and who is inspecting.
  4. Work through each section in order. Tick items as you go. An inspection is not a race, so take your time.
  5. Tap ⚑ Flag on anything that worries you and + Note to jot details such as measurements or a photo reminder.
  6. Check Red Flags & Dealbreakers and log problems in Your Notes with an estimated cost.
  7. Finish with the summary. Export a PDF, save it to your Drive folder, or print it.

Your progress saves automatically on the device you are using. If you look at more than one unit, export the PDF first and then use Reset All, so the details do not blur together.
